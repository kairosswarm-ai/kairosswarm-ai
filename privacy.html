Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy describes how Compound Shock Uploader ("the app") handles Google user data.

What data the app accesses

The app requests access to the owner's YouTube account through Google OAuth with the YouTube Data API scopes needed to upload videos and manage video details (title, description, tags, thumbnails) on the owner's own channel.

How the data is used

The access is used only to upload videos created by the app and to set their details on the owner's own YouTube channel. The app does not read, collect or analyze data about any other user.

Storage

The OAuth credentials (client ID, client secret and refresh token) are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub Actions configuration. No other Google user data is stored.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with or transferred to any third party.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
