Compound Shock Uploader

Compound Shock Uploader is a private tool used by the owner of the Compound Shock YouTube channel. It creates short educational videos about personal finance and business, and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

The tool is used only by its owner. It does not offer sign-in to other users and does not access anyone else's YouTube account.
